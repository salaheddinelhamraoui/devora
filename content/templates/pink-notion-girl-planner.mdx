## Why you will love it

**It is designed, not decorated.** The pink is applied through a consistent palette rather than sprinkled on, so the workspace stays readable during a long study session.

**Nothing is locked.** Change the palette, swap the covers, delete the sections you do not need. Once it is duplicated, it is yours.

**It travels.** Layouts hold up in the Notion mobile and tablet apps, so you can check deadlines from the bus.

## How it works

1. Complete checkout. One payment, no subscription.
2. You get an email with a PDF containing your private Notion link.
3. Open the link and press **Duplicate** in the top-right corner.
4. The planner copies into your own Notion workspace, ready to use.

A free Notion account is all you need. This is a digital product, so nothing is shipped.
