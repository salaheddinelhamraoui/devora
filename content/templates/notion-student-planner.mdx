## Why students stick with it

**It is calm.** No neon dashboards or forty-button home pages. Soft neutrals, generous spacing, and a layout that does not punish you for falling behind.

**It is editable.** Every database, view and page is unlocked. Rename a course field, delete a section you do not need, restyle it to match your vibe.

**It is beginner friendly.** If you have never used Notion, you will still be running this in a couple of minutes. Duplicate, and it works.

## How it works

1. Complete checkout. Payment is one time, no subscription.
2. You get an email with a PDF containing your private Notion link.
3. Open the link and press **Duplicate** in the top-right corner.
4. The whole planner is copied into your own Notion workspace. Start using it.

A free Notion account is all you need. This is a digital product, so nothing is shipped.
