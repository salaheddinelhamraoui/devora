## Why it works

**You only enter data once.** Every dashboard reads from the same databases, so the numbers are always consistent.

**It shows the uncomfortable truth kindly.** Category breakdowns and subscription totals make overspending obvious without turning finance into a chore.

**It bends to your setup.** Different currency, different categories, different pay cycle: rename anything, add anything, delete what you do not use.

## How it works

1. Complete checkout. One payment, no subscription.
2. You get an email with a PDF containing your private Notion link.
3. Open the link and press **Duplicate** in the top-right corner.
4. The full budget system lands in your own Notion workspace.

A free Notion account is all you need. This is a digital product, so nothing is shipped.
